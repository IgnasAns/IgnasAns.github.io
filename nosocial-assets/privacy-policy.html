No Social — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-11 (contact updated 2026-08-28)

This policy explains, in plain language, what the No Social app does with your information. It is a general summary — it is not legal advice.

The short version

  • No Social works fully offline. Blocking apps and tracking focus sessions needs no internet connection.
  • No account. No sign-up. No profile. There is nothing to log in to.
  • Your usage data (sessions, streaks, stats) stays on your device. It never leaves your phone, unless you choose to share a focus card.
  • Usage events are recorded locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. No Social never sees your card details.
  • We do not sell your data. We have no data to sell.
  • Delete the app and all of its data is deleted with it.

What the app does

No Social is a focus tool. You choose the apps you want to stay away from, choose a session length, and commit. While a session is active, opening a blocked app shows the block screen instead. When the session ends, you get your stats, your streak, and a shareable focus card.

Information we collect

Data that never leaves your device

  • Your block list (which apps you chose to block)
  • Session history (start and end times, durations)
  • Streaks and daily/weekly stats
  • App preferences (durations, goals)

This data is not transmitted anywhere. It exists on your phone and nowhere else.

Analytics

No Social records lightweight usage events (sessions started and completed, streaks) locally on your device only, in the app's private storage. They are not linked to you and are never transmitted. The current version makes no analytics network requests at all.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Emergency Escape consumables and the lifetime Pro option) are handled by Google Play. No Social never receives, stores or processes payment card details. All the app learns from a purchase is that it succeeded, so it can unlock the feature.

Focus cards you share

If you share a focus card (an image showing your stats), that image is created on your device and shared through whatever method you choose. No Social does not see or store what you share.

Permissions and how they are used

Usage access (Android)

Used to detect when you open an app on your block list, so the block screen can appear. No Social sees only the names of apps and when they were opened. It does not read the content of any app.

Accessibility service (Android)

Used for one thing: to detect when a blocked app is opened and to show the block screen. The service does not read, store or transmit screen content, keystrokes, text or any other data. All processing happens on your device.

Draw over other apps (Android)

Used to display the block screen on top of other apps while a session is active.

Notifications (Android)

Used to tell you when a session ends, and to send reminders you opt into.

Ignore battery optimisation (Android)

Optional. Prevents the system from killing No Social while a block session is active.

Children

No Social is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data deletion

No Social keeps no server-side data about you. Deleting the app from your device deletes all data — there is nothing stored anywhere else.

No data sold

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data. We do not show ads and do not use advertising identifiers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data (access, correction, deletion, export). Because No Social does not collect personal data, these rights are satisfied by design: there is no personal data for anyone to access, export or delete. Contact us at the address below with any question or request. This is a general summary of rights under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA and EU GDPR; it is not legal advice.
