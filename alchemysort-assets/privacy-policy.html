Alchemy Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Summary

Alchemy Sort is fully offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your game progress, settings, and streak history are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (hint packs, undo packs, supporter upgrade) are processed entirely by Google Play. The app never sees or stores your payment details.

Permissions

The app requests only Vibrate (haptic feedback on puzzle actions). It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13+ and contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no external links.
