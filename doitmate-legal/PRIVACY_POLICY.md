# Privacy Policy for Do It Mate

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Do It Mate ("the app") helps small groups keep each other to their goals. This
policy says what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

- **Account.** Your email address and password if you create an account (the
  password is handled by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain
  text), or nothing at all if you use a guest account. Your display name.
  Optionally a profile photo and a payment link, which is a web address you
  choose to share so friends know where to send what you are owed.
- **What you create in the app.** Goals, check-ins, optional proof photos and
  notes, comments, group chat messages, and the record of penalties and
  payments between group members.
- **Notifications.** A push-notification token for your device and its
  platform, so the app can send reminders and group activity. You can turn
  reminders off in the app (You, then Reminders) or in your phone's settings.
- **Timezone.** Your device's timezone, so deadlines and the weekly recap follow
  your own day.
- **Purchases.** If you subscribe to Do It Mate Pro, Google Play takes the
  payment. We receive a purchase token and confirm it with Google to switch Pro
  on. We never see your card details.
- **Usage and diagnostics.** Basic events (for example, opening a screen or
  checking in), the app version and crash information, linked to your account,
  to fix problems and see what works.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, an advertising identifier or
payment card details.

## Who can see what

- **Your groups.** Members of a group you join can see your name, photo, goals,
  check-ins, proof photos, chat messages and balances in that group. Public
  challenges are groups anyone can join, so what you add there is visible to
  the people taking part.
- **Nobody else.** There is no public feed and no way to browse other people's
  data.
- **Service providers.** We use Supabase (database, authentication, file
  storage and server functions), Expo (delivery of push notifications) and
  Google Play (billing). They handle data only to provide those services.
- **We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.**

## Money

Do It Mate keeps a record of penalties that friends agree between themselves.
It does not process, hold or transfer money. Payments happen outside the app.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the
app: You, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, goals,
check-ins, proof photos, chat messages and any balances you owe or are owed.
Groups you created are handed over to another member; a group where you are the
only member is deleted. A guest account is deleted when you sign out.

If you can no longer open the app (for example, you uninstalled it), contact us
using the details under Contact below and we will delete your account and its
data on request.

## Children

Do It Mate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes,
tell you in the app.

## Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the Do It Mate page in Google Play.
