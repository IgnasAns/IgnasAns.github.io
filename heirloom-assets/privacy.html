Privacy Policy

Heirloom: Life Simulator (com.ianskaitis.heirloom)
Last updated: 13 September 2026

Short version: Heirloom is an offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

1. Who we are

Heirloom: Life Simulator is developed and published by IADevelopment ("we", "us"). You can reach us at info@iaengineering.net.

2. What data we collect

None. The game does not collect or share any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically:

Data typeCollected?Detail
Name, email, phone, addressNoThere is no account and no sign-up.
LocationNoLocation permission is not requested and not used.
Contacts, photos, files, mediaNoNo such permission is requested.
Device or other identifiersNoNo advertising ID, device ID or fingerprinting.
App activity / analyticsNoNo analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included.
Health or fitness dataNoIn-game health values are fictional and stay on your device.

3. Where your game is stored

Your game progress, settings and achievements are stored locally on your device only (in the app's private local storage). Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. You can also erase it from inside the game (Settings → Delete save).

4. Advertising

Heirloom contains no advertising of any kind. There is no advertising SDK in the app and no advertising identifier is read or used.

5. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. When you make a purchase, Google processes the payment and tells the app whether the purchase succeeded; the app stores only a local flag for what you have unlocked. We never receive your name, card details, billing address or any other payment information. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Internet permission

The app declares the Android INTERNET permission solely because the Google Play Billing library requires it to talk to the Play Store on your device. The game itself is fully offline: it works with no connection at all and sends nothing to any server.

7. Children

Heirloom is intended for players aged 13 and over and contains mature fictional themes (including death, illness and crime). We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Your data lives on your device and you control it: deleting the app, or using the in-game delete-save option, removes it completely.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Any future version will continue to describe an app that does not collect personal data unless we state otherwise, clearly and prominently.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@iaengineering.net.
