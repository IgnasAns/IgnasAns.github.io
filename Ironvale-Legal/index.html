IRONVALE — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 (applies to version 1.5.0 and later)

IRONVALE ("the App") is a single-player medieval sandbox game published by ANSKAITISGROUP S.L. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, who receives it and why.

Short version: your game progress is saved on your device and is never sent to us. The App shows optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between battles (Google AdMob), unless you buy Remove Ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your answer at any time from the title screen.

1. Game progress (on your device)

Your characters, saves and settings are stored in the App's local storage on your device. We have no account system and no game server: nothing of this is sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus, and short ads at natural breaks (for example after a battle report). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostics and crash information of the ad SDK,

in order to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we use Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested; if you do not consent, no personalised ads are shown. You can reopen the form with the ads & privacy button on the title screen. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Players who buy Remove Ads or Royal Patron no longer see ads between battles and are never required to watch one. More: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, a Starter Pack, silver purses, companies, Royal Patron) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive from Google Play only the confirmation that a purchase was made, so that the App can unlock it; the App keeps a record of what you bought on your device. Refunds and order history are handled by Google Play.

4. Permissions

The App asks for no special permission. It uses the network only to show ads, to show the consent form and to process purchases. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Children

IRONVALE is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game contains stylised medieval combat; the ad content rating is limited to teen-appropriate ads.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Consent to personalised ads can be changed on the title screen (ads & privacy) where the law requires it to be offered.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes the game data on your device.
  • You can ask us any question about your data using the contact below. Because we hold no personal data about you, we usually have nothing to delete, but we will answer every request.
