ParentShield Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Summary

ParentShield is a parental-control app that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics.

What ParentShield accesses

To enforce screen-time limits, ParentShield uses three Android capabilities, all enabled by you during setup:

  • Usage access — reads which apps are open and for how long, so daily time limits can be enforced and reports can be shown. This data never leaves the device.
  • Accessibility / block service — displays the full-screen "time's up" screen when a limit or bedtime is reached. Only the foreground app's name is read; ParentShield never reads screen content, messages, photos, or keystrokes.
  • Display over other apps — lets the "time's up" screen appear instantly on top of any app.

All limits, schedules, profiles and the parent PIN are stored locally on the device in app-private storage. The PIN is stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text.

Subscriptions

ParentShield Pro subscriptions (optional) are processed entirely by Google Play. ParentShield itself never sees or stores payment information.

Children's privacy

ParentShield is designed for parents to configure on a device used by their child. No information about children is collected, stored off-device, or shared with anyone.
