Zombie Front — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Summary

Zombie Front is fully offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your game progress, squad upgrades, and unlocked heroes are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other personal data.

Children

The app is rated for ages 13+ and contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no external links.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: info@iaengineering.net
