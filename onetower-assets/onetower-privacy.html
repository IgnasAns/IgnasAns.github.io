Privacy Policy — ONE TOWER

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Overview

ONE TOWER ("the App") is a tower-defense / incremental game published by ANSKAITISGROUP S.L. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, who receives it and why.

Short version: your game progress is saved on your device. The App shows optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the game is played and how our ads perform. If you choose a leaderboard name, your name and best scores are published on the world leaderboard and in tournament brackets. Purchases are processed by Google Play. You can change your ad and analytics consent any time in Settings → Ad privacy → CHOICES.

1. Game progress (on your device)

Waves reached, upgrades, currencies, cards, settings and what you own are stored in the App's local storage on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the App or reset your progress in Settings.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App offers optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers (for example the app set ID),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostics and crash information of the ad SDK,

for showing, measuring and personalising ads and for fraud prevention. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; if you do not consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Players who bought Disable Ads or the VIP Pass never see ads.

3. Analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to measure our advertising campaigns (Google Ads). It collects:

  • app interactions: sessions, screens and game events such as tutorial completion, waves and tiers reached, tournament entries, shop visits and completed purchases (product and price as reported by Google Play),
  • an app-instance identifier and, where you allow it, your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language) and approximate location (country/region derived from the IP address).

We do not send your leaderboard name or any free text to Google Analytics. Analytics follows the same consent choice as ads: until you consent (where consent is required), Google Analytics runs in a restricted mode without storing identifiers for analytics or advertising. Event-level data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (currently 2 months); aggregated reports are kept longer. Google acts as our service provider; see How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. World leaderboard and tournaments (optional)

If you set a leaderboard name, the App sends to our server (iaengineering.net, hosted by Hostinger in the EU):

  • the name you chose,
  • a random installation ID created by the App (it is not linked to your device, account or advertising ID),
  • your best tier and wave, and your best wave in the current tournament.

Your name and scores are shown publicly to other players on the world leaderboard and in your tournament bracket. Your IP address is processed by the server to deliver the request and may appear in the host's security logs. Without a name nothing is sent and tournaments are played against computer rivals only. Entries are kept while the board is running; tournament brackets are deleted after 35 days. To have your entry removed, email us with your leaderboard name.

5. In-app purchases

The App offers optional one-time purchases and an optional auto-renewing subscription ("Tower Pass"). Every purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or payment credentials; Google provides us only the confirmation needed to deliver the item. Subscriptions renew until cancelled in the Google Play Store app (Subscriptions). "Restore purchases" re-applies earlier purchases on a new device with the same Google account. Google's handling of payments is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — ads, analytics, the leaderboard and Google Play billing.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — Google Play purchases.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and Android's ad-services permissions — used by AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase as described above, subject to your consent and device settings.

The App does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts or storage permissions.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw your ads/analytics consent: Settings → Ad privacy → CHOICES in the App.
  • Stop leaderboard sharing: clear your name and we will delete your entry on request.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal data we hold, object to processing, or complain to your data protection authority. The legal bases we rely on are your consent (ads personalisation and analytics where required), and our legitimate interest in running and improving the game and its leaderboard.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the App's data practices change; the effective date at the top shows the latest version.
