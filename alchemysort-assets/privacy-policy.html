Alchemy Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version: Alchemy Sort has no account and no login. Your progress is stored on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob (you are asked for consent where the law requires it) and measures anonymous usage with Google Firebase Analytics. Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your name, address or card details.

1. Who we are

Alchemy Sort ("the App", package com.iaengineering.alchemysort) is a puzzle game developed and published by ANSKAITISGROUP S.L. ("we", "us"). Contact for anything privacy-related: info@iaengineering.net

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, vials, themes, the elements you discovered, the daily streak, purchases already made and your settings) is stored only on your device. We do not upload it and hold no copy. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App deletes it permanently; we cannot restore it or delete it for you because we never receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you remove ads with an in-app purchase, the App shows banner ads, ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information (model, operating system, app version) and information about the ads you see and interact with. We do not receive this data and do not combine it with anything else.

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App shows Google's consent form on first launch, before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Your controls on Android. You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. You choose to watch one for a reward (for example a hint or double coins); declining costs you nothing.
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Analytics (Google Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played so that we can fix problems and balance levels: events such as "level started / completed", "hint used", "ad watched" and "purchase completed", together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language and approximate country. These events do not contain your name, email address or anything you typed. Where consent is required, measurement stays limited until you have answered Google's consent form, and follows your answer.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers optional one-time purchases: the Alchemist Starter Kit, No Ads Forever, hint packs and coin packs. There are no subscriptions.

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; Google is the merchant of record and handles your payment details. We do not receive or store your payment method, billing address or name. The App only receives the confirmation that a purchase succeeded so that it can deliver the item.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.
  • Restore purchases (Settings) re-delivers permanent items, such as No Ads Forever, on a new device with the same Google account.
  • Coins, hints, vials and other in-game items are virtual and have no monetary value; they cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to other people.

6. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: ads, analytics and purchases.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Google Play purchases.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets the ads and analytics libraries read the advertising ID, subject to your consent and Android's ad settings.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback (can be switched off in Settings).

The App does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or calendar.

7. Children

Alchemy Sort is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to info@iaengineering.net and we will delete it.

8. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

We do not hold personal data about you: your progress stays on your device, and ad and analytics data is processed by Google under its own policies. To exercise your rights over data Google holds (access, deletion, objection, opting out of personalisation), use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy and the Android ad settings above, or change your consent in Settings → Privacy choices.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is closed. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority; in Spain that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

9. International transfers and security

Google may process ad and analytics data outside your country, under the safeguards described in Google's policies. We run no server that stores player data. Traffic between the App, Google Play and Google's services uses HTTPS.

10. Changes to this policy

We update this page whenever the App's data practices change, and the date above changes with it.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: info@iaengineering.net
