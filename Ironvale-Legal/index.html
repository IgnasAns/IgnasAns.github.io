IRONVALE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

IRONVALE is a single-player, offline medieval sandbox game. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data. The short version: we do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data.

Data collection

IRONVALE collects no personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party code that transmits data. It contains no in-app purchases and no social features.

Device data and storage

All game progress is stored locally on your device (browser local storage inside the app). IRONVALE makes no network connections of any kind and works entirely offline. Nothing is ever transmitted from your device.

Permissions

IRONVALE requests no special permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.

Children

IRONVALE is suitable for players aged 10 and up. Because no data is collected, there is no special handling required for children's data.

Contact

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
