Ardenhold privacy policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Ardenhold is an offline castle sim. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

  • No account. There is no sign-in, profile, cloud sync or online service.
  • No game servers. The game never contacts a server of its own. The Android app includes Google's Play Billing library for its one optional purchase; that library needs the internet and network-state permissions to talk to the Google Play Store app on your device. The game sends nothing through it.
  • One optional purchase. The Full Campaign is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The app keeps only a note, on your device, that the campaign is unlocked. Google's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking. There are no ad SDKs, telemetry or crash-reporting SDKs, and nothing else that phones home.
  • Local files only. The save slot, campaign ledger and settings are stored on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • No personal data is requested. The game does not ask for a name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

If you contact the developer through the store listing, the store's own messaging system handles that conversation under the store's privacy terms. Nothing from it is added to the game.

The game contains stylised medieval combat: units fall and buildings collapse, without blood or gore. It is intended for a general strategy audience.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address shown on the store listing.

Changes to this policy will be published with a new effective date in the store listing and in the repository.
