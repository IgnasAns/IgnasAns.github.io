Privacy Policy for RepRace

Last updated: 30 October 2026

RepRace ("the app") counts your exercise reps with your phone's camera and lets you compete with friends. This policy says what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Camera

The camera picture is analysed on your phone to find your body position and count reps. Camera video and photos of you exercising are never recorded, stored or uploaded. Only the result (the number of reps, and when the set started and ended) is saved.

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address, used to send you a sign-in code. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google.
  • Profile. Your username, and optionally a display name, country, fitness goal and fitness level, and a profile photo you choose.
  • Date of birth. Used only to check that you are old enough to use the app. Nobody else can see it.
  • Workouts. For each set: the exercise, the number of reps (or seconds, for holds) and the start and end time.
  • Social. Your friends and friend requests, the groups you create or join, and the challenges you create or take part in.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, an advertising identifier or any payment details. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see what

  • Other users can see your username, display name, country, profile photo, and your rep totals on leaderboards and in challenges you are part of. They cannot see your individual workouts, your email address or your date of birth.
  • Users under 18 are left out of the public (global) leaderboards.
  • Service providers. We use Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and Google (Google sign-in and on-device pose detection through ML Kit). They handle data only to provide those services.
  • We do not sell your data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, workouts, friendships, the groups and challenges you created, and your place in other groups and challenges.

If you can no longer open the app, contact us using the details under Contact below and we will delete your account on request.

Health and safety

RepRace is for fun and fitness, not medical advice. Exercise within your limits, warm up, and stop if you feel pain or dizziness. Make sure the space around you is clear before you start.

Terms of use

  • You must be at least 16 years old to use RepRace.
  • Keep it friendly: no offensive usernames or photos, no cheating, and no harassing other users. We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.
  • The app is provided as is. Rep counts are automatic and can occasionally be wrong.

Children

RepRace is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the RepRace page in Google Play.
