Privacy Policy — Epoch Legion: Time War

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Overview

Epoch Legion: Time War ("the App") is a strategy game published by ANSKAITISGROUP S.L. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, who receives it and why.

Short version: the App has no accounts and we run no game servers. Your progress is saved on your device. The App shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played. Purchases are processed by Google Play. You can change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy options and switch analytics off in Settings → Share usage data.

1. Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings are saved locally on your device. Android may include this save in your device backup to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup). Uninstalling the App deletes the local save. We cannot see or access it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show, limit and measure ads, Google AdMob may process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app-set ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, clicks, completed rewarded videos) and SDK diagnostics.

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and where required by law, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The App sends pseudonymous gameplay events (for example "battle started", "chest opened", "purchase started"), your account level and Timeline, an app instance ID, device model, OS version, country and session information, so we can fix problems and improve the game. Analytics starts with all Consent Mode signals denied and follows your consent choice. You can turn it off in Settings → Share usage data.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms; we never receive your payment details. The App keeps purchase tokens on your device so it can deliver and restore what you bought.

5. Notifications

Optional reminders (for example "your chest is ready") are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the App or in Android settings.

6. Remote configuration

The App may download a small settings file (event dates, feature switches) from a static web host. Nothing about you is sent; the host processes your IP address to deliver the file.

7. Permissions

Internet, Google Play Billing, advertising ID and (Android 13+, only if you allow it) notifications. The App does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD). Because we hold no account for you, most data stays on your device or with Google; email us and we will help, including with deletion requests for analytics data linked to your app instance ID. Legal bases: your consent (personalised ads, analytics) and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the App.

10. Retention

Local saves stay until you uninstall the App. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics for 14 months. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies.

11. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
